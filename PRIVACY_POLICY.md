## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how My Inventory ("the App"), developed by [Your Name/GitHub Username] ("we", "us", or "our"), handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

## Data Collection and Storage

**The App does not collect, transmit, or store any data on external servers.** All data is stored locally on your device only.

### Information You Provide

The following information is stored exclusively on your device:

- **Account credentials**: Username and password (password is hashed using SHA-256 before storage)
- **Instrument details**: Type, make, model, serial number, condition, and estimated value
- **Photos**: Images you select from your device's photo library

### Information We Do NOT Collect

- We do not collect analytics or usage data
- We do not use tracking technologies or cookies
- We do not collect location data
- We do not access your contacts, calendar, or other personal data
- We do not transmit any data over the internet

## Device Permissions

The App requests the following device permissions:

- **Photo Library Access**: Required to allow you to attach photos of your instruments. Photos are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded or shared.

## Data Storage

All data is stored using your device's local storage mechanisms:
- On iOS and Android: AsyncStorage (on-device only)
- On Web: Browser localStorage (on-device only)

**No data leaves your device.** There are no servers, cloud storage, or external databases involved.

## Data Security

- Passwords are hashed using SHA-256 before being stored locally
- All data remains on your device and is not accessible to us or any third party
- Data security is dependent on your device's own security measures (passcode, biometrics, etc.)

## Data Deletion

You can delete your data at any time by:
- Deleting individual instruments from within the App
- Uninstalling the App, which removes all locally stored data
- Clearing the App's data through your device's settings

## Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics platforms, advertising networks, or external APIs.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The App stores no personal data externally and has no age-gated content.
